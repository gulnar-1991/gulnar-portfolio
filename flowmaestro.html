AI automation tools are powerful, but hard to understand.

FlowMaestro is a platform for building AI workflows and agents. Tools like Stack AI can do a lot, but they often use technical language and dense screens.

For a first-time visitor this creates real problems:

  • Complex ideas are hard to explain quickly.
  • The screens feel overwhelming.
  • The value is not clear. People ask: what does this product actually do?

My goal: design a landing experience that turns complexity into clarity.

Simplify how it is explained, not what it can do.

The design keeps the technical depth of the product, but shows it in clear, structured sections.

  • Clarity over complexity

    Break AI workflows into short, easy sections.

  • System-based layout

    Modular sections that mirror how workflows and agents are built.

  • Light and dark

    Test both themes to find the right feel for different audiences.

Direction first, then handoff.

My scope was the design direction, so I focused on structure and clarity before polish. I worked in four steps.

  1. Wireframe

    Set the structure and content order before any visual design.

  2. Simplify

    Turn complex AI ideas into short, clear sections.

  3. Design

    Build high-fidelity screens in both light and dark themes.

  4. Hand off

    Share the Figma direction early with developers.

Two directions, one product.

I explored a light and a dark theme to decide how the product should feel. Use the switch to compare the same page at each stage. Scroll inside the frame to see the whole page.

Clear enough to build from.

I worked with the development team on the real product. They reviewed the Figma direction early and confirmed it was clear enough to build from without any extra screens.

What this shows: I can design product direction, not only UI. I explain complex systems clearly, build structures that scale, and support real development work.
