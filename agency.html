Big agencies chase big brands. Small shops get left behind.

A handmade bag maker, a massage therapist, a painter, a security installer. They are good at their craft, but they have no time, no budget for an agency and no wish to learn ten tools.

Generating an image or a caption with AI is easy. The hard part is judging it: is it on brand, is the product shown honestly, will it bring a customer?

The goal: an agency that does the work, checks its own work, and only asks the owner to tap "yes".

A team of agents with a quality gate.

I designed the system like a small agency: 20 specialised agents with clear jobs, from researcher and brand director to video director and analytics. Every piece of work goes through the same loop before a person sees it.

  • Make

    Agents write, design and edit from the client's brand file and real product photos.

  • Check

    Machine checks first: sizes, text length, claims, repeats.

  • Judge

    An AI creative director scores the work. Below the bar, it retries up to 3 times.

  • Approve

    Only then the owner sees it, as a card they tap in chat or Telegram.

  • Publish

    Scheduled posting to Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn through APIs, with daily checks and missed-post alerts.

  • Learn

    Every correction becomes a written rule the system checks next time.

The agency website.

I designed and built the site with GSAP and Lenis: a clear promise, a work grid, and a case page for each client in that client's own colours. The audit form sends each request to my phone and email, then starts a draft audit automatically.

Personal outreach, made by a system.

Cold messages full of text get ignored. So every prospect gets a 30-second video about their own business: their real Google results, the problem, and the fix, in my own cloned voice.

I built it as a template. One small file per prospect holds the script and the facts. The system makes the voice, renders the motion design frame by frame with sound effects, makes a contact sheet to check itself, and publishes a personal page. About two minutes per video.

For a security installer I went further: a personal page with a working instant-estimate form, a support menu and an after-hours hotline demo, so the owner can try the fix before buying it.

What I learned

Taste has to be measured. "Make it like this reel" fails. Breaking the reference into frames, cut times and a transcript first, then checking my render against it, works.

Honest visuals sell. Product shots must show the real item, whole. Posts that showed a face got 111 views against 11 to 21 for the rest.

Selling beats building. I froze new features until a client paid, and shipped client work instead.
