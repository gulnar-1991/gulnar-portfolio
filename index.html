About me

My journey into product design has been shaped by a strong foundation in 3D design, visual storytelling, and creativity. Before transitioning into UX/UI, I spent several years working with tools like Maya, ZBrush, and Substance Painter, where I learned to think deeply about form, structure, and how ideas come to life visually.

Over time, I became more interested in how people interact with products — not just how they look, but how they feel and function. This led me to shift into product design, where I could combine visual thinking with user experience and problem-solving.

Today, I design digital products that focus on clarity, usability, and thoughtful interaction. I also build AI products and generative creative workflows.
